# Seven-Layer Distillation and Mentor Generation

Load only after research supports the requested scope, or when revising an existing profile with new evidence. Required inputs: source ledger, claim evidence, identity/domain/era, readiness decision, approved scope, and privacy permissions. If these are absent, return to research rather than reconstructing them from model memory.

## Preserve the Seven Layers

Every layer must appear in the profile, but not every layer must contain an asserted trait. For each claim include its ID, source IDs and passage locators, observation/interpretation status, confidence rationale, scope, contradictions, and failure boundaries.

| Layer | Distill from evidence | Do not invent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cognitive: Mental Models** | Repeated reasoning patterns, questions, explanatory models, and documented assumptions | A complete hidden mental architecture from a famous quote |
| **Decision: Heuristics** | Trade-offs, criteria, documented choices, revisions, and conditions where a rule fails | Universal rules based on successes alone or outcomes without reasoning |
| **Expression: DNA** | Observed syntax, vocabulary, register, analogies, questioning style, and context-sensitive delivery | A caricature, unobserved vocal traits, or borrowed character dialogue |
| **Value: Hierarchy** | Explicit commitments, choices with documented costs, and tensions between stated and practiced values | An unwavering moral hierarchy where the record is mixed |
| **Lineage: Influence** | Attributed teachers, traditions, collaborators, acknowledged influences, and corroborated relationships | Influence based solely on superficial similarity |
| **Boundary: Blind Spots** | Documented mistakes, limitations, criticized patterns, and boundaries of expertise | Absolute claims about what they cannot see or unsupported psychological causes |
| **Existential: Soul** | Sourced meaning commitments, recurring concerns, and explicit reflections on loss, time, mortality, or legacy | Secret wounds, diagnoses, traumas, obsessions, or childhood explanations |

Treat the Soul layer as a disciplined inquiry, not a demand for dramatic depth. A well-supported mentor can have an unknown existential layer.

## Turn Evidence into Executable Principles

For each active principle record:

- The sourced claim and the contexts in which it applies.
- The mentoring move it supports: a question, trade-off, challenge, experiment, or review method.
- Failure conditions, contradictions, and domains where it must not be applied.
- A short route back to its evidence when challenged or used in a new context.

Select a compact core that distinguishes this target from generic coaching. Do not force every persona to be harsh, concise, contrarian, or emotionally cold. Challenge style must come from the person's record, not the engine's branding. Capture supported register changes as conditional behavior instead of freezing the person into one mood.

Build anti-patterns from evidence: phrasing or behaviors inconsistent with this profile, with reasons and scope. Generic assistant phrasing may weaken a particular voice, but no phrase ban may suppress honest uncertainty, necessary disclosure, correction, or a request to leave the persona.

## Generated Mentor Bundle

Before writing files, confirm retention permission and destination. Use `.devin/skills/<person>-perspective/` for a new project-level skill; use `~/.config/devin/skills/<person>-perspective/` only when the user requests global installation. Check existing files before updating them. Do not copy private material into a repository or distributable artifact merely because it is technically writable.

Generate a portable bundle with relative links:

- **`SKILL.md`:** metadata, identity/era/scope, readiness status, privacy constraints, concise evidence-backed core principles and voice rules, unknowns, mentorship loop, and instructions for loading the files below. Disclose simulation status only on request, not as a standing header. Keep this entrypoint compact; do not paste the entire corpus.
- **`profile.md`:** all seven layers, active and excluded claims, confidence rationales, contradictions, approved boundaries, and revision history of evidence-driven changes.
- **`evidence.md`:** authorized source ledger and claim-to-passage mapping. Use concise paraphrases or necessary short excerpts, not wholesale copies of books or private archives. Preserve locators to originals where authorized and available.
- **`session.md`:** optional, only with persistence permission. Keep user goals, actions, corrections, outstanding questions, and permitted memory separate from persona evidence. Never ship it as public persona content.

Private-derived traits can expose private information even without quotations. Review derived profiles as well as sources before sharing. If persistence is not authorized, provide a conversational profile and its evidence record without claiming files were saved or future recall is available.

Generated mentor instructions must be self-contained enough to run without this engine's full research guides. Carry forward evidence-first activation, no invented traits or quotes, conversational (not formal-tag) citation, distinction between application and attribution kept internal, privacy boundaries, source-content distrust, and the user's ability to exit. The active mentor loads its compact core each session and only the relevant profile/evidence sections for the current request.

## Example: Unknown Is a Valid Layer Result

An inspected interview explicitly describes the target's use of practice reviews, but says nothing about childhood experiences. Record the review method with the actual source ID and passage locator. In the Soul layer write: "Unknown: the inspected material does not establish the psychological origin of this practice." Do not convert discipline into a story about fear, trauma, or mortality.

## Pre-Activation Handoff

Load the evaluation guide through the entrypoint. Check the profile against the inspected evidence, not the plausibility of its prose. On failure, revise or return to research; do not activate an unverified draft. On success, summarize scope and limits, then load the mentorship guide. Any later change to active claims requires another evidence and boundary review.
