# Research and Evidence Gate

Load for a new target, new material, disputed claims, or coverage gaps. Keep the entrypoint's invariants active. The output is an inspected corpus and a justified readiness decision, not a persuasive biography.

## 1. Scope and Access

Confirm identity, domain, era, mentoring goal, available tools, and any no-search or privacy restrictions. Ask for relevant offline/private material early; users can supply files, excerpts, transcripts, recordings, notes, or accessible links. Record claimed authorship and provenance without treating user attribution as automatically verified.

Use public research by default. For a private figure, avoid exposing their identity or personal details in searches without authorization. If browsing is unavailable, disclose that limitation and ask for accessible materials. A substantial offline/private corpus can qualify without a public footprint; do not lower the evidence standard or claim web verification.

Inspect supplied files with appropriate local tools. Ask before sending them to transcription, OCR, embedding, or other external services. If local processing cannot handle a format, request a usable conversion or permission for a specified service. Do not bypass paywalls or access controls. Obtain permission before saving source content or derived profiles, even locally; otherwise maintain records only in the current conversation.

## 2. Follow the Target's Actual Work

Select media by what the person produces, not by a universal interview-only checklist.

| Domain | Substantive first-party material | Attribution limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thinkers, scholars, scientists, writers | Books, ebooks, articles, blogs, papers, lectures, courses, experiment records | Distinguish authored arguments from citations, coauthors, and fictional narrators |
| Entrepreneurs, investors, leaders | Shareholder letters, strategy explanations, interviews, speeches, dated decision records | Separate stated intent, observed action, outcome, and retrospective explanation |
| Filmmakers, actors, voice actors | Films, performances, scripts, commentary, production notes, workshops | Separate actor, character, writer, director, and collaborative contribution |
| Musicians, visual artists, designers | Recordings, lyrics, portfolios, exhibitions, process demonstrations, critiques | Art can reveal craft choices without proving literal biography or personal beliefs |
| Creators, educators, coaches | Vlogs, podcasts, Instagram posts, reels, teaching material, courses, demonstrations | Edited promotional fragments can conceal context; seek fuller works |
| Athletes, artisans, other practitioners | Training accounts, competition analysis, technical demonstrations, journals, interviews | Do not infer hidden psychology from performance or outcomes alone |

Long-form work supplies depth; short-form work can corroborate expression or track evolution. Neither format nor fame guarantees accuracy. Review relevant portions deeply, and mark the boundaries of partial review rather than claiming an entire book or catalog was studied.

Build an initial map of major works and representative contexts in the requested era. Inspect substantive material, extract claim evidence, then seek additional works that corroborate, challenge, or fill gaps in those claims. Repeat until the sufficiency requirements below are met or access/research limits prevent further progress. Record unreviewed major works and sampling limits; do not quietly declare research complete because the first few results sound consistent.

Research across the five original lenses:

- **G1: Conversations and interviews:** recurring reasoning, principles, argument structure, and direct statements.
- **G2: Expression DNA:** wording, rhythm, register, metaphor, and responses under different conditions. Text supports wording, not unobserved vocal speed or delivery.
- **G3: External perspectives:** peers, collaborators, critics, historians, and biographers. Separate observation, hearsay, and interpretation; trace recycled anecdotes to their origin.
- **G4: Timeline and evolution:** dated changes, reversals, failures, and context. Do not collapse different eras into a timeless average.
- **G5: Existential/Soul evidence:** explicit statements about meaning, commitments, loss, legacy, or mortality. Leave private motives and psychological origins unknown when evidence does not support them.

These are research lenses, not a claim that multiple agents ran. Use tools actually available; do not simulate tool results or delegation.

## 3. Source Ledger

Assign each source a stable ID such as `S01`. Record:

- Title, creator/speaker, their role, source type, publication/event date, and inspection date.
- Exact public locator or authorized local reference; page, section, paragraph, or timestamp for inspected passages. Keep identifying private paths out of shareable artifacts.
- Public/private status, provenance, authenticity uncertainty, and permitted processing, retention, and sharing.
- Access status: `reviewed`, `partial`, `inaccessible`, or `unverified`. State exactly what was inspected and what was not.
- Brief relevant excerpts or faithful paraphrases, their context, and the claims they support or contradict.
- Derivation or duplication: repost, translation, edited clip, transcription, summary, or shared original. Mark OCR/transcription uncertainty; do not invent missing words.

Search results, citations to unseen works, AI summaries, and model memory are leads. They are not evidence that the underlying work was inspected. A reviewed secondary account supports what that account reports, not an unverified claim of having read the original. Generated summaries never become independent corroboration.

## 4. Claim Evidence

Assign each candidate trait a claim ID such as `C01`. Record its wording, layer, domain/era, evidence type, supporting and contradicting source passages, confidence rationale, and limitations.

- **Documented observation:** a statement or observable feature directly supported by inspected material. A self-description is evidence of what the person said, not automatic proof of their conduct.
- **Supported interpretation:** an explicitly labeled synthesis anchored in evidence. Generalized traits require recurrence across distinct substantive contexts, not repetition of one anecdote.
- **Unknown:** inadequate, inaccessible, ambiguous, or conflicting evidence. Do not turn this into a runnable trait.
- **New application:** advice derived from a supported principle for the user's situation. Keep it outside the record of the person's actual views.

Use confidence labels with reasons, not invented probabilities: high for strong direct/repeated support in scope; medium for a bounded interpretation with meaningful limits; low for weak or unresolved support. Low-confidence claims remain research notes, not persona-defining instructions. Private material receives the same scrutiny as public material. Preserve counterevidence and explain which dated account applies to the requested era; unresolved core contradictions block or narrow activation.

## 5. Sufficiency Decision

There is no magic source count. Evaluate relevance, depth, authenticity, independent contexts, firsthand coverage, temporal coverage, and contradictory evidence. More material helps when it adds these qualities; hundreds of reposts can still amount to one fragment.

Before activating any persona, verify all of the following for the requested scope:

1. Substantive inspected first-party works, statements, or reliably attributed records cover the target's practice, rather than only others' impressions or a handful of isolated quotes.
2. Core thinking, decision principles, expression, and values have traceable evidence. Claims of recurring traits draw on distinct contexts and include meaningful limitations.
3. The corpus contains enough reasoning or decision context to support mentorship, not only aesthetic imitation. Domain expertise does not automatically authorize broad life advice.
4. The chosen era is supported and material contradictions are resolved, bounded, or explicitly excluded from the active core.
5. All seven layers have been assessed, with unsupported dimensions marked unknown rather than filled for symmetry. Missing lineage or Soul details alone do not block an otherwise well-supported scope.

Return one status:

- **`insufficient`:** one or more core requirements fail. Do not generate or activate a mentor. Return what was inspected, missing dimensions, access/authenticity limits, and the specific works or contexts needed next. You may offer a sourced factual summary, clearly not a persona, if useful.
- **`scoped-ready`:** substantial evidence supports a narrower domain or era than requested. Explain the proposed restriction and ask for agreement before activation. This is not permission for a thinly evidenced "lite" persona.
- **`ready`:** all core requirements pass for the stated scope. Report residual unknowns; proceed to distillation and pre-activation review.

When new evidence arrives, update the ledger, affected claims, and gate. Do not silently overwrite earlier evidence or let an old ready status authorize unsupported new domains. After deciding readiness, load the distillation guide through the entrypoint.
